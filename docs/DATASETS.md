## Matched normal-tumor liver

GEO series GSE70090. Processed archive: `GSE70090_RAW.tar`.
Four matched normal/tumor liver pairs were analyzed, each with conventional BS-seq and oxBS-seq.
Reference genome: hg19/GRCh37. Annotation: GENCODE v19 comprehensive chromosome GTF and UCSC hg19 `cpgIslandExt.txt.gz`.
